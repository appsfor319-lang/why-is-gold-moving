      How the explanation is built

      • Gold must move at least 0.15% or the app says it is quiet.
      • Each input counts only if it moved enough to matter: dollar 0.1%, 10-year yield 3 basis points, silver 0.3%, oil 2%.
      • Two or more inputs in gold’s direction: stated as the reason. One: marked tentative. None: the app says it cannot establish why.
      • Daily data is never presented as live. Each input would carry its own timestamp.
