TRANSPARENT ANALYSIS

How the analysis is built

Start with gold

The briefing begins with today's gold move and the actual price change, then looks for evidence explaining it.

Only gold-related news

The newsdesk searches for gold, bullion and precious-metals coverage. Macro or geopolitical stories are included only when relevant to gold.

Connect the causes

The analysis checks yields, the dollar, silver and oil, then weighs the headlines against the market reaction.

Separate fact from interpretation

Every feed carries its own timestamp. If evidence is mixed or missing, the briefing says so instead of inventing certainty.

Independent evidence review

Market prices, economic releases and published reporting are compared. A headline is not treated as proof that it caused a gold move.

Respect data freshness

Daily macro data can lag live prices. Stale readings are labelled and excluded from the explanation instead of being presented as current market moves.
