How the briefing works

Cross-market confirmation

Gold is compared with silver, DXY, US 10-year yields and crude oil. Confirming signals are separated from conflicts.

News is evidence, not proof

Headlines link to publishers. Timing and market reaction must support a catalyst assessment.

Honest confidence

Missing, stale or contradictory feeds lower confidence. Correlation is not treated as certainty.

Update schedule

The open browser checks the API every 15 minutes. A separate scheduled cache can be added on the backend.
